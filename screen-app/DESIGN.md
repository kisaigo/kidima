## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a premium, highly structured aesthetic for a marketplace connecting clients with master craftspeople, renovation specialists, and emergency repair artisans. Drawing functional restraint from Linear, architectural balance from Stripe, clinical clarity from Doctolib, and welcoming hospitality from Airbnb, the design language balances utilitarian engineering with artisanal prestige.

### Brand Personality & Core Mood
- **Architectural & Disciplined:** Utilitarian, reliable, clean grid alignments, zero unnecessary visual clutter.
- **Prestige Craftsmanship:** Deep forest greens, warm gilded accents, tactile materials, and balanced white space convey trust, excellence, and mastery.
- **Frictionless Utility:** High legibility, rigorous data density, and instant legibility for stressful emergency workflows or meticulous interior renovation estimates.
- **Zero Emojis:** Information clarity is maintained purely through structured typography, deliberate microcopy, and refined monoline geometric iconography.

### Design Movement
- **Precision Modernism:** Combines crisp structural borders, micro-elevation shadows, and surgical spacing metrics. Surfaces rely on pure optical balance, soft borders, and warm contrast rather than flamboyant decorative artifacts.

## Colors

The palette establishes authority, craft trust, and structural clarity. It pairs an earthy, refined botanical depth with crisp architectural neutrals and a warm honey-amber accent.

### Color Tokens & Semantic Roles
- **Primary (`#14532D`):** Deep forest green. Applied to core call-to-action anchors, active indicators, verified artisan badges, and structural brand moments.
- **Primary Dark (`#0F3820`):** Deep shadowed pine. Used for button hover states, authoritative banners, and high-emphasis focal items.
- **Primary Soft (`#F0FDF4`):** Soft botanical wash. Utilized for positive feedback banners, selected filter states, light badge fills, and calm informative highlights.
- **Accent Warm (`#D97706`):** Warm burnished gold/amber. Reserved exclusively for star ratings, artisan certifications, curated mastery badges, and conversion micro-accents.
- **Neutral Primary / Text Dark (`#0F172A`):** Deep obsidian slate. High-legibility text baseline with deliberate warm softness compared to raw `#000000`.
- **Neutral Secondary / Text Slate (`#64748B`):** Neutral gray-blue slate. Used for metadata, breadcrumbs, placeholder values, and supplementary craft details.
- **Background Canvas (`#F9FAF8`):** Organic neutral ivory-tinted mist. Softens eye fatigue across wide display surfaces.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container surfaces separating dense content cards, modal sheets, and toolbars from the canvas.
- **Border Subtle (`#E2E8F0`):** Hairline structure line framing layout modules, cards, and input boundaries.
- **Border Interactive (`#CBD5E1`):** High-contrast border for unselected form inputs, inactive tabs, and table dividers.

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on **Hanken Grotesk** across display, body, and micro-labels to achieve modern European precision. Its crisp terminals, geometric construction, and high x-height evoke architectural documentation and structural blueprints.

### Hierarchy & Typesetting Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked headings (-0.03em to -0.015em) provide cohesive, editorial authority.
- **Numeric Clarity:** All financial transactions, quote estimates, and hourly fees use tabular numeric formatting (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to align digits perfectly across tables and booking summaries.
- **Scale Compression:** Mobile screens compress display sizes past 32px down to a baseline maximum of 32px or 28px to preserve UI canvas space and avoid unwanted line wraps in artisan titles.
- **No Emoticons or Generic Glyphs:** Standard textual labels paired with hairline SVG icons handle all status, warning, and category representation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an uncompromising 4px / 8px numerical increment system: `[4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40]`.

### Grid System
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column fluid grid, 16px outer margin, 16px gutter. Content flows vertically with compact bottom-sheet workflows for booking and emergency calls.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column fluid grid, 24px outer margin, 24px gutter. Split screens activate for map exploration and artisan profile cards.
- **Desktop (≥1024px):** 12-column fixed-max grid (max container width: 1280px), centered with 40px outer margins and 32px gutters.

### Spacing Application Rules
- **`space-2xs` (4px):** Inline icon-to-label spacing, segmented control inner padding.
- **`space-xs` (8px):** Chip internal padding, form input vertical padding, adjacent text badges.
- **`space-sm` (12px):** Compact card inner cell gaps, compact list item vertical spacing.
- **`space-md` (16px):** Standard card padding, standard input field height cushion, default component separation.
- **`space-lg` (20px):** Form fieldset groupings, stacked list card gaps.
- **`space-xl` (24px):** Card interior section dividers, modular component container padding.
- **`space-2xl` (32px):** Section-to-section layout spacing on mobile screens, modal body interior padding.
- **`space-3xl` (40px):** Desktop section separation and hero block clearance.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through subtle, low-opacity ambient shadows combined with structural 1px borders. Elevated surfaces avoid harsh drops; depth mimics natural light diffused through a glass skylight over clean architectural planes.

### Depth Layers
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F9FAF8`. Zero shadow. Used for the fundamental application canvas background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Surfaces):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` and micro-elevation:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
  Used for artisan listings, trade service cards, and search filters.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Interactive Cards):**
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
  Elevates items on pointer hover, indicating physical tactile readiness.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts, Popovers & Dropdowns):**
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
  Used for calendar booking menus, artisan craft specialty pickers, and filter dropdowns.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Fixed Bottom Sheets):**
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.10), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
  Used for emergency dispatch confirmations, estimate details, and quote request drawers.

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette reflects an architectural, purposeful modernism. Border radii map to the requested scale: `[8px, 12px, 16px, 20px]`.

### Radius Distribution
- **8px (`radius-sm`):** Buttons, input fields, dropdown trigger buttons, segmented switches, and status chips.
- **12px (`radius-md`):** Artisan summary cards, review cards, inline service tiles, media previews.
- **16px (`radius-lg`):** Feature highlight cards, pricing proposal cards, map preview windows, dialogue overlays.
- **20px (`radius-xl`):** Mobile sheet containers, hero booking pods, and primary drawer modules.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Verification badges, pill search categories, online dispatch status dots.

## Components

- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, radius `8px`. Hover: background `#F8FAFC`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Background `transparent`, text `#0F172A`, hover: background `#F0FDF4`, text `#14532D`.
- **Emergency / Urgent Dispatch:** Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, left accent ring in `#D97706`. Height `48px` on mobile for priority access.

- Height `44px`, radius `8px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Padding horizontal `16px`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#14532D`, box shadow ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.12)`.
- **Placeholder:** Text `#64748B`, font size `15px`.

- **Artisan Avatar:** Square with `12px` radius (never circular, reflecting modern architectural framing), bordered with `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Badges:** Placed top-right with `8px` radius, background `#F0FDF4`, text `#14532D`, border `1px solid rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Category Filters
- Height `36px`, radius `8px` or pill `9999px`.
- **Default:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`.
- **Active / Selected:** Background `#F0FDF4`, border `1px solid #14532D`, text `#14532D`, font weight `600`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `20px x 20px`, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `6px` for checkbox, circular for radio.
- **Selected:** Background `#14532D`, border-color `#14532D`, white geometric check mark or dot. Focus ring matching the primary green.

### Lists & Activity Rows
- Dividers are subtle `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Row padding vertical `12px` or `16px`.
- Interactive rows transition to background `#F8FAFC` on hover.

### Star Ratings & Badges
- Iconography: Monoline geometric star with solid fill `#D97706`. Score printed in `numeric-data` style with `#0F172A`, review count enclosed in parentheses in `#64748B`.
